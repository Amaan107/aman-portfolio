About Me

I'm a Computer Science student at COMSATS University with a deep passion for building elegant, user-centric digital products. My journey started with curiosity about how websites work and evolved into a genuine love for frontend engineering and mobile development.

I believe great software is invisible — it just works. That philosophy drives every line of code I write, whether it's a responsive web interface, a Flutter app, or a backend API. I'm always learning, always building, and always pushing to ship cleaner, faster, more accessible experiences.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me exploring new technologies, contributing to open-source projects, or dissecting the UI/UX of products I admire. I'm actively looking for opportunities where I can grow alongside experienced engineers and make a real impact.
